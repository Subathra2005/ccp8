20. (a) Anthropometric measurements are measurements of the human body
        used to assess growth, nutritional status and body composition.

    (b) Various anthropometric measurements include:

        -   Body weight
        -   Standing height or crown-heel length
        -   Body circumferences, such as MUAC
        -   Skinfold thickness/body fat measurement

    (c) Dietary assessment involves reviewing the food and nutrient
        intake of an individual or population and comparing the intake
        with reference values to identify possible deficiency or excess.
        Common methods include 24-hour dietary recall, food
        diary/record, diet history and food frequency questionnaire.
